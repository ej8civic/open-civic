## Rules this wiki follows

- **No source, no number.** Every spec cites an approved source and page.
- **One task per page.** Short pages, filtered by chassis code, engine and market.
- **`verified`** means someone did the job on a real car.

> Community documentation, not affiliated with Honda. Work safely: use jack stands, let hot parts cool, and check specs against your own manual.
