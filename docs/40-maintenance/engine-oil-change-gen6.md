# Engine oil change (Gen 6, 1996-2000)

The most important job for a long-lived Civic. Clean oil at the right level protects the bearings, the cam and the VTEC system.

> **Applies to:** EJ6 · EJ7 · EJ8 · EM1 · US <br>
> **Difficulty:** 1/5 · **Time:** 45 min · **Safety:** medium (hot oil, car on stands)

<br>

## Context

- Change the oil warm: it drains faster and carries more dirt out.
- Hot oil burns. Loosen the drain bolt carefully, with gloves.
- Honda allows replacing the filter every *other* change under normal use. For an old engine, replacing it every time is cheap insurance.
- D16Y8 and D16Y5 take less oil than the D16Y7. Check your engine code before buying oil.

> VTEC (D16Y5, D16Y8, B16A2) is switched by oil pressure. Low or dirty oil can stop VTEC from engaging.

## Steps

```mermaid
graph LR
  A[Warm up engine] --> B[Car level & secured]
  B --> C[Drain oil]
  C --> D[New washer, torque drain bolt]
  D --> E[Swap filter, 7/8 turn]
  E --> F[Refill to spec]
  F --> G[Run 3+ min, check leaks]
  G --> H[Recheck dipstick]
```

1. Warm the engine to operating temperature, then switch it off.
2. Park on level ground. If you lift the car, use the factory lift points (SM section 1, p. 1-41) and jack stands. Never work under a car held only by a jack.
3. Remove the drain bolt carefully and let the oil drain fully into the pan.
4. Fit a **new washer** and tighten the drain bolt to spec. Do not overtighten: the pan threads strip easily.
5. Remove the filter with the wrench. Wipe the engine seat clean.
6. Put a thin film of oil on the new filter's rubber seal. Screw it on by hand until the seal touches, then turn it **7/8 turn** more.
7. Refill with the capacity for your engine. Start slightly under and top up.
8. Run the engine for more than 3 minutes, then check the drain bolt and filter for leaks.
9. Switch off, wait a few minutes for oil to drain back, and check the dipstick. The level must be between the upper and lower marks.

## Checklist

- [ ] Engine code confirmed (see [VIN & model codes](../10-identify/gen6-us-vin-codes.md))
- [ ] New drain bolt washer fitted
- [ ] Drain bolt torqued (39 or 44 N·m)
- [ ] Filter seal oiled, 7/8 turn
- [ ] No leaks after 3 min run
- [ ] Level between dipstick marks
- [ ] Mileage and date written down
